## 16 研究想法

**Agent-derived candidate:** Affordance decomposition of recruitment AI. Hypothesis: explanation changes investment targeting without necessarily improving immediate selection, whereas active tasks change both learning and evidence production. Delta: separate information, guidance, and intervention channels. Validation: factorial toggles rather than only sequential R0-R3 contrasts. Falsifier: channel-specific toggles do not reproduce regime contrasts. Failure modes: toggles may violate realistic system bundles; effects may depend on arbitrary uptake parameters. Innovation status: prior-art search required.
