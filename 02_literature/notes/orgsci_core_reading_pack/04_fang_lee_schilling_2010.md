## 02 一句话总结

The paper revises the idea that more connectivity necessarily improves learning by modeling semi-isolated subgroups, showing that moderate cross-group links can maximize equilibrium performance by diffusing superior ideas without eliminating diversity too quickly. [Paper: Abstract]

## 14 学到的知识

The valuable result is not “networks matter,” but that the same link creates a benefit through diffusion and a cost through premature homogenization. [Analysis]

## 15 与已有知识的连接

[Analysis] AI feedback may act like dense cross-group linking: standardized explanations spread the same view of valuable skills, improving alignment while shrinking diversity. This is a plausible mechanism for a fit-versus-option-value trade-off.

## 16 研究想法

**Agent-derived candidate:** Feedback homogenization. Hypothesis: more standardized AI explanations improve short-run target alignment but concentrate investment into fewer skills and reduce long-run adaptability under demand change. Delta: replace interpersonal diffusion with common institutional feedback. Validation: vary feedback personalization and environmental shift while holding information accuracy fixed. Falsifier: standardized feedback improves fit without reducing diversity or post-shift recovery. Failure modes: diversity may be normatively irrelevant; personalization may change signal accuracy inadvertently. Innovation status: prior-art search required.
